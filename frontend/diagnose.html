Leaf doctor: photograph a leaf of any plant. The app names the plant, checks if it is healthy or sick, and tells you what to do. For maize, potato, tomato and cassava a specialist model also names the exact disease.

How it works: CLIP (an image-and-text deep learning model trained on 2 billion pictures) checks that the photo is a plant, names it among about 120 African crops, fruit, trees and weeds, and compares it with descriptions of healthy and sick leaves; a small layer trained on 15 plants' labelled photos (PlantVillage; iCassava 2019, Makerere University AI Lab) sharpens the healthy/sick check. It is tested on plants it never saw in training. The specialist CNN covers maize, potato, tomato and cassava. A photo is a first check, not a lab test: for anything serious, show the plant to your extension officer.
