How farmers use it without a smartphone

Most farmers have a basic phone. They dial *384# and pick from numbered menus in English, Kiswahili or French, or they send an SMS keyword in any of 11 languages:

  • BEI MAHINDI KITALE: price now, in 3 months, sell or store
  • MBOLEA MAHINDI KITALE: planting dates and fertilizer per acre
  • BIMA MAHINDI KITALE: insurance price and this season's status

For real phones, a USSD/SMS gateway (for example Africa's Talking) is pointed at /api/ussd and /api/sms. Answers longer than one USSD screen (182 characters) are sent in full by SMS.

Plan: soil maps + 35 years of rain + QUEFTS. Insurance: weather index learned from real district harvests (HarvestStat Africa). Prices: graph neural network on live WFP data. Guidance, not a guarantee.
